  </div>
  <div class="col-12 col-sm-8 col-md-9">
    <h3>Sampath Gamage</h3>
    <p><em>Assistant Professor of Nanoscience</em></p>
    <p>Dr. Gamage joined the Department of Nanoscience at the Joint School of Nanoscience and Nanoengineering (JSNN) at UNC Greensboro in Fall 2025. His research combines adaptive functional materials, oxide electronics, organic–inorganic hybrid thin films, nanofabrication, device physics, and nanoscale characterization. He earned his Ph.D. in Physics from Georgia State University and previously held research appointments at the University of Georgia and Linköping University in Sweden.</p>
