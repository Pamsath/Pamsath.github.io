        <p class="card-text"><em>Assistant Professor of Nanoscience</em></p>
        <p class="card-text">Sampath Gamage leads the Gamage Lab in the Department of Nanoscience at the Joint School of Nanoscience and Nanoengineering, UNC Greensboro. His research combines adaptive functional materials, oxide electronics, organic–inorganic hybrid thin films, nanofabrication, device physics, and nanoscale characterization. He earned a Ph.D. in Physics from Georgia State University and previously held research appointments at the University of Georgia and Linköping University.</p>
